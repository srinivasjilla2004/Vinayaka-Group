VINAYAKA GROUP OF COMPANIES — MULTI-PAGE CORPORATE PORTAL
Exact Design & Dropdown Navigation Inspired by ARC Group (arcgroup.co.in)
================================================================================

1. ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW:
   The website is organized into separate, dedicated pages:
   
   - index.html        : The Group Portal Home (Hero Slider, Portfolio Overview, Stats, Client Ticker)
   - yamaha.html       : Dedicated Yamaha Two-Wheelers Dealership (Models, Blue Square, Specs, Booking)
   - cfa.html          : Dedicated Vinayaka Corporation C&F Agency (Warehousing, Logistics, Client Brands)
   - gopuram.html      : Dedicated Gopuram Traditional Products (8 Devotional Items, WhatsApp Orders)
   - our-story.html    : Dedicated Corporate Profile & Leadership (MDs T.V.K. Chaitanya & J. Prabhakara Rao)
   - reach-us.html     : Dedicated Locations & Contact Page (Interactive Google Maps for all 4 Showrooms)

2. ASSETS STRUCTURE:
   - assets/css/style.css : Shared extraordinary ARC Group stylesheet (dropdown animations, orange-red active bar, typography, responsive grids)
   - assets/js/main.js    : Shared interactive JavaScript (navbar scroll effects, mobile drawer, modal, counter animations, tab switcher)
   - vinayaka_logo.jpg    : Official company logo from your upload

3. ARC GROUP NAVBAR & PORTFOLIO DROPDOWN:
   - Header with brand logo on left and navigation on the right:
     HOME | OUR STORY | PORTFOLIO ⌵ | CLIENTS | LEADERSHIP | REACH US
   - Hovering "PORTFOLIO ⌵" reveals the exact dropdown menu from the ARC Group reference:
     * 🏍️ Vinayaka Yamaha (Blue Square Dealership) -> yamaha.html
     * 📦 Vinayaka Corporation (C&F Agency & Logistics) -> cfa.html
     * 🪔 Gopuram Retail (Devotional & Pooja Products) -> gopuram.html
     * 🏬 Vinayaka Warehousing Hub (Iron Yard Complex) -> cfa.html#warehouse
     * 🔧 Yamaha Certified Service & Spares -> yamaha.html#service
   - Active Orange-Red Underline Bar underneath the current tab.

4. HOW TO PREVIEW:
   - Double-click index.html or open any page in your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
   - Click between pages or use the PORTFOLIO dropdown menu.
