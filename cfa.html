Infrastructure & Capabilities

End-to-End Carrying & Forwarding Excellence

Vinayaka Corporation is an established logistics leader located at the landmark Iron Yard Complex in Bhavanipuram, Vijayawada. We serve as the vital supply chain link for leading FMCG, confectionery, cosmetics, and pharmaceutical brands across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

High-Capacity Warehousing

Heavy-duty, secure industrial warehouses equipped with modern racking, pest protection, and 24/7 surveillance.

Secondary Fleet Distribution

Coordinated transportation network ensuring rapid dispatch to stockists, distributors, and wholesalers.

Skilled Operations Manpower

Certified material handlers, stock supervisors, and logistics personnel managing high daily throughput.

Zero-Loss Inventory Tech

Digital batch ledgering, FIFO adherence, expiry protection, and real-time reconciliation reporting.
